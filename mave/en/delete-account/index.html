MAVE · Service Factory

Delete your account in the app

When you are signed in, you can delete your account directly from the account screen in MAVE.

  1. Open MAVE and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the account screen.
  3. Select Delete account near the bottom of the screen.
  4. Review what will be deleted, then confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion by email

If you deleted the app or cannot sign in, email servicefactory10@gmail.com. To help us verify your identity, include the app name MAVE and the email address you used to register or sign in.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your email address, social sign-in identifiers, authentication information and other data linked to your sign-in account will be deleted.
  • Any remaining clean-export balance and its connection to your account cannot be recovered.

Data that may be retained

  • Minimum transaction records required for legal compliance, purchase verification, refunds and fraud prevention may be separated from your sign-in account and retained only for the necessary period.
  • MAVE does not upload original or completed videos to its servers. Videos and editing drafts stored on your device are not deleted when you delete your account. You can delete them directly from your device.

Contact

For questions about account or data deletion, email servicefactory10@gmail.com.
