Privacy Policy

MAVE · Service Factory · Effective September 8, 2026

MAVE processes the videos you select on your device. MAVE does not upload original or completed videos, thumbnails, audio, file names, capture dates or times, or location data to its servers.

1. Information we process

  • Information processed only on your device: Videos you allow MAVE to access or select directly, capture information, date ranges, scene selections, editing settings, and editing drafts.
  • Optional account information: Your email address, Apple or Google sign-in identifier, and account and session information required for authentication. You do not need to sign in to create free videos with the MAVE mark.
  • If clean-export purchases are offered: Store platform, transaction and product identifiers, clean-export quantity, purchase, verification and refund status and timestamps, a randomly generated project identifier, and clean-export usage history. MAVE does not store your payment card information or original store receipt.
  • Support information: Your email address and the contents of a support request when you contact us by email.

2. How we use information

  • To import, edit, combine, save and share videos, and restore editing drafts
  • To create accounts, sign users in, maintain sessions and protect accounts
  • To maintain clean-export balances, verify purchases, prevent duplicate credit, and process use, recovery and refunds
  • To answer support requests, investigate errors, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations

3. Service providers and international processing

MAVE does not sell or share personal information for advertising. We may use the following providers to operate the service:

  • Supabase for account authentication, databases and server functions
  • Apple and Google for social sign-in and app-store payment processing

Information independently processed by these providers is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Information may be processed on systems located outside your country. MAVE limits transfers to what is necessary and encrypts information in transit.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Videos, completed videos and editing drafts stored on your device remain there until you delete them from the app or your device.
  • Account information is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Minimum transaction records required for legal compliance, purchase verification, refunds and fraud prevention may be separated from your account and retained for the period required by applicable law, then deleted.
  • Support requests are deleted after they are resolved unless applicable law requires retention for a specific period.

5. Your choices and rights

You can change photo and video access in your device settings and delete drafts and results stored on your device. To access, correct or delete account information, use the account screen in MAVE or contact us at the email address below.

View account and data deletion instructions

6. Security

MAVE uses encryption in transit, secure device storage for sessions, restricted server access and user authentication. We minimize the information we process by never sending original media to MAVE servers.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when features or applicable laws change. We will provide notice of material changes in the app or on our distribution page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email servicefactory10@gmail.com.
