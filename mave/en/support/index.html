Moments are valuable. · Service Factory

MAVE creates a memory video by joining one second from each video recorded on a selected day or date range. Original and completed videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to MAVE servers.

What can we help with?

  • Importing videos
  • An unexpected result
  • Saving or sharing
  • The MAVE mark or clean exports
  • Accounts and sign-in
  • Feature requests

Video access and saving

MAVE uses only videos you allow it to access or select directly. You can change photo and video permissions in iPhone or Android settings. MAVE does not modify or delete original videos. A completed video is added to your photo library only when you choose to save it.

Accounts and sign-in

You can create free videos with the MAVE mark without signing in. You can sign in with Apple, Google or email. If you have trouble signing in, include the sign-in method and error message in your support request. Never send your password or verification code.

Delete your account

Signed-in users can delete their account from the account screen in MAVE. If you cannot sign in to the app, you can request deletion by email using the instructions below.
